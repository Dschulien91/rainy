Privacy policy

In short

rainy works without an account. Signing in is optional; it syncs your favourites and settings across devices and is needed for rain and thunderstorm alerts. We only set cookies that are technically necessary (chosen language, sign-in). Your location is only used to show the weather for that place and is sent to our server rounded to about 1 km. We do not pass on any data to third parties.

What data is processed

Location. With your permission the app asks for your device's location. For weather, radar and place names it sends the coordinates rounded (weather and radar about 1 km, place name about 100 m) to our server. The server answers the request and does not store it; answers are cached without any reference to you so the same places are not calculated again. Our server looks up place names and the place search at Photon (komoot) or Open-Meteo – without your IP address or device data.

Server logs. Our server does not store IP addresses or access logs with personal data. For technical reasons our hosting provider Hetzner Online GmbH (Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany; servers in Germany) and the content delivery network Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) see the IP address when a page is loaded (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, secure and fast operation). Data processing agreements (Art. 28 GDPR) are in place with both. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; data may also be transferred to the USA.

Language cookie. If you switch the language, a cookie "rainy_lang" stores your choice for one year so the start page opens in that language (technically necessary, § 25 (2) TDDDG).

Settings and favourites stay on your device only (browser storage) and are not transmitted unless you sign in.

Rain and thunderstorm alerts (optional, account required). Alerts require signing in (see "Account and sign-in"). If you turn them on, our server stores, linked to your account: the places to watch (rounded to about 1 km), your alert settings (lead time, heavy-rain heads-up, thunderstorm alerts, quiet hours with start and end time) and, for each device on which you allow notifications, the push address the browser creates for this, your time zone and – for "My location" – the location last seen on that device (rounded to about 1 km). The alert settings are synced between your devices like your favourites. The message is delivered via your browser's push service (e.g. Google, Apple, Mozilla). A device's push address is deleted when you sign out on that device; all alert entries are deleted when you turn off all alerts or delete your account (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, consent). Alerts set up without an account before sign-in was introduced keep running without a link to an account until you turn them off or the push service invalidates the address.

Map. The map images come from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org); this service sees your IP address as with any image request. With the map design "Satellite", images are loaded from NASA (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov).

Email

If you write to hey@hellorainy.com, we only use your message and address to reply. Emails are stored at our email provider ALL-INKL.COM (Neue Medien Münnich, Hauptstraße 68, 02742 Friedersdorf, Germany).

Account and sign-in (optional)

If you sign in, we store your email address, for Google sign-in also the account ID Google sends for rainy (no name, no profile picture), your synced favourites and settings (including the alert settings), your alert entries if any (see above), and your active sessions (only as a non-reversible hash). The legal basis is providing the account (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). For sign-in we set the technically necessary cookies "rainy_session" (sign-in, 180 days) and "rainy_in" (only a hint that you are signed in, without content) (§ 25 (2) TDDDG).

Sign in with Google. You are redirected to Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland); Google learns that you sign in to rainy and sends us your email address and an account ID. Google's privacy policy also applies.

Sign in with an email link. We send you a sign-in link via our email provider ALL-INKL.COM. The link is valid for 15 minutes and can be used once.

Deletion. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account); your email address, synced data, sessions and all alert entries with their push addresses are deleted immediately. Favourites and settings on your device stay.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Write to hey@hellorainy.com. Alert entries are linked to your account; older alert entries without an account can only be identified and deleted via the app (turn off alerts).
