Running the app

When you open the app, our host Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) and the delivery network Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) process your IP address for technical reasons (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). We have data processing agreements with both; Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The app uses your location only with your permission, to show the weather there; it is sent rounded to our server and not stored there. Settings and favourites stay on your device. Technically necessary cookies and browser storage (language, sign-in, settings) need no consent (§ 25 (2) TDDDG). With the map style "Satellite" the app loads images directly from NASA.

Google Analytics

Only if you consent, we use Google Analytics by Google Ireland Limited (Ireland) to understand how the app is used. It sets cookies and sends usage data (e.g. pages viewed, device, approximate origin) to Google, also to the USA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework). The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 25 (1) TDDDG); you can withdraw it at any time in the cookie settings.

Account and alerts (optional)

For an account, sync and rain or thunderstorm alerts we store your email address, with Google sign-in also your name and profile picture, your favourites, settings, alert places and your device's push address (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). Alerts are delivered via your browser's push service. Sign-in emails and mail to us go through ALL-INKL.COM (Germany). You can delete your account at any time in the settings; we then delete this data.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Write to us at hey@hellorainy.com.
