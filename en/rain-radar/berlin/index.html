    Rain radar Berlin – rain in the next 2 hours

    Is it about to rain in Berlin? The rain radar shows live where it is raining around Berlin right now and where the rain clouds will move in the next two hours. The map is centred on Berlin (population 3,426,354), and the timeline below shows to the minute when the rain starts and when it stops.

    Live radar for Berlin and the surrounding area

    It is based on the radar images of the German Weather Service (DWD), updated every five minutes. From the direction the rain areas are moving, rainy forecasts Berlin in 5-minute steps. Light drizzle that the radar cannot see is added from the DWD weather stations in Berlin.

    Weather in Berlin: 24 hours and 3 days

    Beyond the next two hours, rainy shows the DWD weather models for Berlin: rainfall, temperature and cloud cover for the next 24 hours and three days.
